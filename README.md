# Proposal-for-Building-a-Database-of-1000-Target-Companies

If I were required to build a database of 1000 companies, I would follow a structured process to ensure consistency and quality.

First, I would clearly define the target company profile, including industry segments, company size, business model, and level of technical capability. This would help avoid collecting companies that do not fit the objective.

Next, I would divide the work industry-wise. For example, industrial automation, robotics, medical devices, energy systems, process engineering, automotive components, and other manufacturing sectors. This would make the search process more organized.

I would then use sources such as Google Search, Google Maps, LinkedIn, industry directories, startup directories, and company websites to identify potential companies. The information collected would be maintained in Excel with fields such as company name, website, industry segment, city, products, revenue band (where available), and decision makers.

After collecting the data, I would evaluate each company using a scoring framework based on relevance, technical capabilities, innovation, growth potential, and overall alignment with the target profile.

The highest-scoring companies would then be reviewed manually to verify that the information is accurate and that the company genuinely fits the required criteria.

This process would make it possible to build a database of 1000 companies in a systematic and repeatable manner while maintaining data quality.
